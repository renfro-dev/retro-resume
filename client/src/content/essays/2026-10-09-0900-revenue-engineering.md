## WTF is a Revenue Engineer?

A Revenue Engineer combines expertise across three disciplines:

- **RevOps**: trustworthy data, clear processes, and operational accountability
- **GTM engineering**: systems to find, win, and grow the right customers
- **AI enablement**: agents that make these systems more accessible to operators

The role leverages AI to build and maintain the system of record (CRM) while creating systems that enable scalability.

## How Revenue Engineers Get Graded

Success is measured by three metrics:

- Process scalability
- Operational efficiency
- Revenue impact

Everything delivered should layer into enterprise value.

## First Principles of Revenue Engineering

1. **Know when not to build.** Avoid building unmaintainable code; embrace "buy vs build" decisions thoughtfully.
2. **Measure outcomes.** Define which KPIs and OKRs you're moving and establish benchmarks for reliable storytelling.
3. **Define agent autonomy.** Establish where agents act independently, enforce boundaries, and require human approval layers.
4. **Understand change management.** Drive adoption, since impact depends on team adoption of solutions.
5. **Make AI accessible.** Build observability into human-AI relationships for continuous improvement.

## What Revenue Engineers Should Do at Your Company

### 1/ Orchestrate business context into solutions

- Turn AI notetakers into CRM-based business intelligence
- Detect client sentiment and compliance risks
- Identify service expansion and product feedback opportunities
- Flag rep improvement areas regarding product communication
- Automatically create and enrich contacts

### 2/ Deeply understand ideal customers

- Enrich closed-won/lost deals with firmographic data
- Score prospects based on high-value customer profiles
- Identify viable customers in operating geographies
- Create private CRM apps and provision API endpoints

### 3/ Create content customers care about

- Find trending topics via Reddit, X, and niche publications
- Build content publishing pipelines for expert review
- Turn content into outbound and lifecycle campaigns
- Build free tools customers want to use
- Optimize content using SEO/GEO data APIs

### 4/ Build a context harness and structured database

- Set up Postgres databases and write SQL queries
- Implement row-level security
- Select appropriate LLM models and run evaluations
- Leverage data APIs for purchase-timing intelligence
- Maintain structured repositories for LLM reference

### 5/ Tell the business what's coming

- Keep the source of truth clean for reporting quality
- Provide revenue forecasts and pipeline reporting
- Inform other departments on resource allocation
- Help teams operate as ecosystems, not silos

### 6/ Know when to buy vs. build

- Recognize when external solutions beat internal builds
- Identify time-trap rabbit holes to avoid

## What's Next for Revenue Engineers?

The emergence of AI Workspaces (Grok Bot, Dots, Buzz) represents a paradigm shift. People do not want another thing to log into. They want a team of agents with API keys and best practices that they can train like an employee.

These interfaces enable human-AI collaboration in Slack-style environments, moving away from traditional SaaS UIs. This shift empowers daily operators with visibility into workflows previously hidden in "black boxes."

The future is making GTM and RevOps workflows more accessible through AI Workspaces rather than adding more logins.
